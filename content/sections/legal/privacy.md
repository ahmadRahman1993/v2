I'm a senior AI engineer who builds production LLM systems for healthcare, where a wrong answer has real consequences. My focus is evaluation and reliability: designing systems that refuse rather than guess, and measuring formally whether they actually work.

At Virtual Health Hub I built Echo, a RAG assistant for rural health planners, and EchoPCR, a clinical documentation system now in field trial with paramedics. I co-founded Core Syntax, an AI product studio behind SPARK and SREDER, and I maintain reliability-eval, an open-source library for calibration-first LLM evaluation.

I hold an MSc in Computer Science from the University of Saskatchewan and have co-authored two papers in Mayo Clinic Proceedings: Digital Health.
